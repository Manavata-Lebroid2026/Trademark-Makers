FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Trademark Registration FAQs

What is a trademark?

A trademark is a distinctive name, word, logo, symbol or other sign that helps identify goods or services from a particular business.

What is the difference between ™ and ®?

The ™ symbol can indicate a claim to a mark. The ® symbol is used for a registered trademark.

How long does trademark registration take?

Timing depends on examination, objections, opposition and administrative processing. Each application can follow a different timeline.

How long is a registered trademark valid?

In India, a registered trademark is generally valid for ten years and may be renewed.

Can I apply for a logo and a name?

Yes. A word mark and a logo may be filed separately based on the protection you need.
